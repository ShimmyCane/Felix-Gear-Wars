# Books 4 and 5: early ideas (not planned)

These build on what books 1 to 3 already have: gears, buildings and resources, lanes, flanking, and the endless modes.

## Book 4: The Supply Roads

- **A map you travel.** The campaign moves along a road across a region. Each chapter is a town on the road. You keep your army between towns.
- **Supply lines.** Caravans carry food and wood from your farms and sawmills to the army. An enemy raid can cut a road, so the army runs short until it is repaired.
- **Weather and time of day.** Fog hides enemies (archers can't reach them until they are close). Rain slows missiles. Night makes the spellcasters stronger and the archers weaker.
- **Heroes.** One hero per book, who levels up with the army, can lead a lane, and gives a single big move like the skills of book 1.

## Book 5: The Heart Reborn

- **The Heart is a character.** The Great Heart wakes up fully in the last book. The player can ask it for a chosen gear each chapter, at a cost in ticks.
- **Two sides.** Play the Horde for a few levels, with their own buildings and units, so the enemy's problems become visible.
- **The city.** Your buildings join into a town. Each building helps its neighbours (a barracks next to a farm trains faster), so placement matters again, as it did with gears.
- **Ending.** The campaign ends with a crown gear the player must hold back for ten minutes.

Nothing here is decided. Revisit after book 3 has been played.
